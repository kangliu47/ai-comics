# Comic 001: No Driver's License, Full Self-Driving Enabled

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Initial concept

## Core Message

Enterprise organizations are rushing toward autonomous agents before they have taught employees how to use, verify, and safely supervise AI systems.

## Core Critique

AI enablement is treated as optional training while agent platforms are treated as strategic urgency. The result is autonomy handed to people who have not learned how to set objectives, provide context, evaluate outputs, define guardrails, monitor behavior, or take over.

## Vehicle Analogy

AI enablement is driver's education. Agentic platforms are powerful autonomous work vehicles. Human oversight is the ability to take the wheel when automation misbehaves.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. Empty AI enablement classroom. Training topics on the board: "How to prompt," "How to verify," "When to take over," "What could go wrong." Instructor says: "Anyone?"
2. Leadership enthusiastically unveils an "Agent Platform Cybertruck" under a banner: "The Future of Work! Fully Autonomous."
3. A nervous employee who clearly does not understand the controls sits in the self-driving seat. They ask: "Which pedal is 'approve'?" A simple agent-control screen shows: "Objective," "Context," "Guardrails," "Monitoring," "Takeover."
4. The Cybertruck crashes through signs labeled "Wrong Email," "Bad Summary," "Unverified Data," and "Policy Exception." The employee yells: "I thought it was autonomous!"

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: Instructor: "Anyone?" Board: "Prompt. Verify. Take over. What could go wrong."
- Panel 2: Banner: "The Future of Work! Fully Autonomous." Vehicle label: "Agent Platform."
- Panel 3: Employee: "Which pedal is 'approve'?" Dashboard: "Objective / Guardrails / Takeover."
- Panel 4: Employee: "I thought it was autonomous!" Broken signs: "Wrong Email," "Unverified Data," "Policy Exception."

## Punchline Options

- AI tools are powerful. People are essential.
- Enable the driver. Then hand over the steering wheel.
- Autopilot does not eliminate the need for a driver.
- You skipped driving school and bought a robotruck.

## Image-Generation Notes

Use a clean four-panel strip. Make the first panel visibly empty and quiet. Make leadership in panel 2 overconfident but not villainous. The Cybertruck/work vehicle should be angular and simple, not detailed or glossy. The final crash should be absurd but legible, with only a few broken signs.

## Open Questions

- Is "Cybertruck" the right recurring shorthand, or should some strips use "autonomous work vehicle" to avoid brand specificity?
- Should the final caption be a punchline, or should the employee's line carry the joke by itself?

# Comic 003: Waiting for the Cybertruck

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Revised concept; selected draft generated

## Core Message

Useful everyday AI assistants are already available, but organizations often ignore practical value while chasing more glamorous agentic systems.

## Core Critique

The enterprise imagination gets captured by whatever looks most advanced. Meanwhile, simple assistants could already help people summarize, draft, compare, brainstorm, analyze, and accelerate routine knowledge work today.

## Vehicle Analogy

Everyday AI assistants are practical sedans that employees could already drive for routine work. Advanced coding agents or agentic systems are flashy autonomous work vehicles. You do not need a Cybertruck to buy groceries.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. A parking lot contains ordinary AI sedans labeled "Chat Assistant," "Draft Helper," "Research Aid," and "Meeting Notes." A sign reads "Available now."
2. Employees who know how to use the sedans stand at the curb under a sign: "Waiting for Autonomous Agent Cybertruck."
3. One practical employee shrugs, gets into a modest sedan labeled "Useful AI Today," and drives off toward a sign: "Real Work."
4. The waiting group is still staring down the road. A sign says: "Backordered: Revolutionary Future." The sedan has already returned with a draft, summary, analysis, and finished deck.

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: Lot sign: "Available now."
- Panel 2: Employee: "We are waiting for the real AI."
- Panel 3: Practical employee: "I just need to get work done."
- Panel 4: Sign: "Backordered." Sedan trunk labels: "Draft," "Summary," "Analysis."

## Punchline Options

- You do not need a Cybertruck to buy groceries.
- Useful today beats revolutionary someday.
- The future is exciting. The present is still useful.

## Image-Generation Notes

Make the ordinary sedans intentionally boring and effective. The employees are not confused about basic driving; they are trend-chasing and undervaluing practical tools. The humor should come from the contrast between people waiting for something spectacular and one person quietly using the available car. Avoid making the agent vehicle too visually dominant, since it should be absent or delayed.

## Revision Notes

- The selected direction uses workplace deliverables in the sedan trunk while keeping the grocery line as a compact idiom for overbuying capability.
- The current title keeps "Cybertruck" because it quickly signals a flashy, over-discussed vehicle; future variants could switch to "Autonomous Agent Truck" if brand specificity ages poorly.

## Premium Assistant Pilot Connection

This can become a assistant tool fit comic. Integrated Assistant is the company sedan already connected to Microsoft roads. Premium Assistant is the Model Y with autopilot: more capable for some business trips, but not automatically the right vehicle for every errand.

The illustrative pilot version should ask: "Is this a Model Y trip, or does the company sedan already get there?"

## Overlap Watch

Related to Comic 002 on everyday AI adoption, but the failure mode is different. Comic 002 is "everyone got access but not purpose, training, or workflow fit." Comic 003 is "people can already use the practical tools, but dismiss them because the trendier agentic vehicle feels more impressive."

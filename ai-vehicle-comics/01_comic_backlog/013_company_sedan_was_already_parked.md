# Comic 013: The Company Sedan Was Already Parked

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Fictional enterprise assistant pilot scenario

## Core Message

Premium Assistant may be powerful, but Integrated Assistant may already be the right vehicle for Microsoft-native work.

## Core Critique

Users may escalate for Premium Assistant before checking whether an integrated assistant already handles the task inside Outlook, Teams, Office, SharePoint, or other Microsoft-connected roads.

## Vehicle Analogy

Integrated Assistant is the company sedan already parked at the curb and connected to Microsoft roads. Premium Assistant is the Model Y with autopilot. Use the Model Y when the trip needs it, not because the key fob feels more impressive.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. A user asks for a Premium Assistant Model Y key to drive across the corporate campus.
2. The destination sign says "Summarize Teams Meeting" or "Draft Outlook Reply."
3. A company sedan labeled "Integrated Assistant" is already parked on a paved road directly to the destination.
4. The user says, "But the Model Y sounds more strategic."

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: User: "I need the Model Y."
- Panel 2: Destination: "Outlook reply" or "Teams recap."
- Panel 3: Sedan label: "Integrated Assistant." Road label: "Already connected."
- Panel 4: User: "But this feels more strategic."

## Punchline Options

- Check whether the company sedan already gets there.
- Not every errand needs autopilot.
- Tool fit beats tool status.

## Image-Generation Notes

Keep the integrated-assistant sedan boring and clearly useful. The Model Y should look capable but unnecessary for this route. The joke depends on obvious route fit, not tool rivalry.

## Open Questions

- Should the destination be "Teams recap," "Outlook reply," or "SharePoint Q&A"?
- Should the final joke target users, leaders, or rollout planners?

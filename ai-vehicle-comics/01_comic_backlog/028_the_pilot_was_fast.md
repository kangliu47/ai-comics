## Message

A fast pilot on empty roads does not prove that a race-car operating model scales to enterprise commuting. Leadership confuses individual speed with system throughput and discovers the actual objective only after recalling access.

## Analogy

Powerful AI tools are generic open-wheel race cars; resource consumption is fuel and wheel spin; shared integrations and capacity are roads and intersections; coordination is traffic control. Three drivers succeed on an empty network. At office-wide adoption, the same vehicles consume power while shared junctions stop moving.

The critique targets leadership's extrapolation and success metric. Employees remain capable, traffic-control staff remain competent, and the tools retain their individual power.

## Four-panel beat

1. **Broad access, limited uptake:** At a corporate launch, the executive hands a race-car key to one of three interested employees. A tray holds many more keys under EVERYONE DRIVES.
2. **True local success, false scaling claim:** Exactly three race cars pass easily through a four-way signalized crossroad, using distinct approaches and turns. A driver is impressed. The executive announces rollout success into a reporter's microphone.
3. **Shared bottleneck:** Repeat the same crossroad and camera angle, now with queues on several approaches waiting for signal phases. Stopped cars burn fuel with heat/exhaust strokes and foreground wheel spin. The annoyed, competent traffic-control operator gives the worried executive the missing denominator.
4. **Policy reversal:** Repeat the launch stage and handoff framing, now with employees dropping keys into an open KEY RETURNS box under POLICY UPDATE. The executive belatedly names the outcome. A faint distant train is only a background suggestion.

## Pre-production design check — 2026-11-07

- **Core message:** Empty-road pilot speed cannot establish enterprise commuting throughput.
- **Causal chain:** Leadership makes powerful vehicles broadly available → three initial drivers succeed → leadership celebrates too early → office-wide use overloads shared junctions → access is recalled and the metric changes.
- **Visual contrast:** Three cars on open roads versus the same vehicles and roads gridlocked; keys handed out versus keys taken back.
- **Panel logic:** Availability is established in 1, low-load conditions and premature boasting in 2, scale failure and missing denominator in 3, reversal and late objective in 4.
- **Final consequence:** The return box follows directly from the network's inability to move people. It is a policy reversal, with no proof that the next model succeeds.
- **Punchline:** “The cars were fast. The commute wasn't.” distinguishes local capability from the shared outcome.
- **Clutter risk:** Omit FUEL BURN and dashboard labels; use physical wheel rotation and small smoke/heat strokes. Keep two junctions readable and the background train faint.

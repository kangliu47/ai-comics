# Comic 012: Range Is Tokens

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Fictional enterprise assistant pilot scenario

## Core Message

Token usage is energy/range. Good users plan the trip and pack only the context they need.

## Core Critique

Pilot users may burn premium AI capacity by dumping every document, transcript, export, and attachment into context before deciding what question they are actually asking.

## Vehicle Analogy

Tokens are battery charge, fuel, energy, and driving range. Context is cargo. Overloading the vehicle with unnecessary documents burns range before the work begins.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. A Model Y labeled "Premium Assistant" starts with a full battery: "100% token range."
2. A user loads the trunk with boxes labeled "All Emails," "Entire SharePoint," "Six Decks," "Raw Export," and "Old Meeting Notes."
3. The dashboard range drops before the car moves. The user says, "I just need a two-sentence summary."
4. A nearby sign reads "Destination: 3 blocks." The battery reads "12%."

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: Dashboard: "Token range: 100%."
- Panel 2: Cargo labels: "All emails," "Six decks," "Raw export."
- Panel 3: User: "Now summarize one slide."
- Panel 4: Dashboard: "Range: 12%." Sign: "Destination: 3 blocks."

## Punchline Options

- Context is cargo. Tokens are range.
- You used the whole battery backing out of the driveway.
- Bring the map, not the warehouse.

## Image-Generation Notes

Make the range meter visually central. The absurdity should be physically obvious: a small errand buried under excessive cargo. Avoid technical token UI; use simple battery/range imagery.

## Open Questions

- Should the final punchline be "Bring the map, not the warehouse" to connect data and context discipline?
- Should this be vendor-specific or generalizable to all premium AI usage?

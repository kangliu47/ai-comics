# Comic 023: Everyone Gets a Steering Wheel

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Backlog concept for the product orchestration arc

## Core Message

AI democratization still needs product boundaries, routes, escalation paths, and reusable patterns, or citizen development becomes unsupported traffic.

## Core Critique

Leadership can hand every employee AI tools and low-code controls, but without lanes, routes, vehicle classes, destinations, support models, and escalation rules, the organization cannot govern, integrate, or maintain what people create.

## Vehicle Analogy

AI democratization is leadership handing every employee a steering wheel. The AI Product Lead defines where citizen driving is appropriate, when to use standard vehicles, when to escalate to expert teams, and how local work connects to enterprise architecture.

## Audience

AI enablement leaders, citizen development program owners, Data & Analytics product leads, platform teams, and governance partners.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. Leadership hands out steering wheels at an all-hands meeting under a banner reading "AI Democratization."
2. Employees proudly hold wheels while standing in hallways with no lanes, route signs, vehicles, or destinations.
3. Some build improvised carts; others block doorways; a Data Science person receives roadside assistance calls.
4. The Product Lead paints lanes and posts signs: "Citizen build," "Standard pattern," "Analytics Engineering," and "Data Science escalation."

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: Leader: "Everyone gets a steering wheel."
- Panel 2: Employee: "Where do we drive?"
- Panel 3: Support ticket: "Automation stuck in hallway."
- Panel 4: Caption: "Democratized driving still needs traffic rules."

## Punchline Options

- Democratized driving still needs traffic rules.
- Giving everyone a steering wheel did not create lanes.
- Citizen development works better when citizens get a map.

## Image-Generation Notes

The first panel should be simple and absurd: a box of steering wheels handed out like office swag. The final panel should show product routing without overexplaining. Keep the Data Science support ticket as a small background joke if used.

## Open Questions

- Should this comic focus more on citizen developers or leadership's oversimplified democratization message?
- Should "traffic rules" imply governance too strongly, or is that acceptable because product boundaries and governance overlap here?

# Comic 017: Same Problem, Better Engines

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Current selected final: **v5, revised 2026-11-07** with a Data Governance guide clarifying client-dataset landmarks in Panel 2 and fewer, larger queued cars across several intersections in Panel 4. Original readable adaptation delivered on 2026-11-06; v1–v4 remain preserved.

## Message

Local AI software creation succeeds while enterprise coordination infrastructure lags behind, repeating spreadsheet-era fragmentation. Leadership defends app-level functionality despite collective paralysis.

## Analogy

Spreadsheet datasets become competing landmarks named CLIENT, with Sales, Prospects, and Service contexts. A guide wearing DATA GOVERNANCE asks which meaning users need before directing them along a path. This is contextual consistency, not a claim that all legitimate definitions collapse into one dataset. AI lets each team build its own software vehicle; individually functional vehicles block one another across an enterprise road network whose traffic signal is still boxed. Models remain engines, applications are vehicles, governance is traffic coordination, and integration is the road network.

## Four-Panel Beat

1. **Excel:** Two business users proudly display competing client spreadsheets; the manager celebrates empowerment.
2. **Eventually:** The same client datasets become three workbook-shaped landmarks at diverging paths. A guide wearing DATA GOVERNANCE asks whether users mean sales, prospects, or service, then points them toward a route. Two business users listen. No single-source-of-truth cure or heroic rescuer.
3. **AI software:** Two business users launch app vehicles from laptop workstations. The same manager celebrates the new capability. Vehicles have visibly more power and independence than the earlier sheets.
4. **Enterprise network:** An elevated view shows a few dozen individually recognizable cars in distinct queues at several connected intersections. Checked foreground cars face competing directions; two drivers demand priority. The manager defends the working apps beside the still-boxed governance signal. Remove the tiny-car horizon and landscape clutter.

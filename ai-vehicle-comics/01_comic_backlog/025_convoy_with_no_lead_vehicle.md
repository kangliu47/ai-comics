# Comic 025: The Convoy With No Lead Vehicle

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Backlog concept for the product orchestration arc

## Core Message

Enterprise AI teams can move quickly and still fail if their destinations, speed, dependencies, and route choices are not coordinated.

## Core Critique

Leadership often sees motion and mistakes it for alignment. Teams duplicate one another, block shared roads, run out of fuel, or arrive somewhere impressive but irrelevant because no one is coordinating destination, sequencing, and dependencies.

## Vehicle Analogy

The enterprise AI program is a convoy with no lead vehicle. The AI Product Lead becomes the lead vehicle and dispatcher, coordinating route, pace, dependencies, and enterprise destination.

## Audience

AI program leaders, Data & Analytics product leads, enterprise portfolio owners, architecture teams, and executives overseeing many parallel AI efforts.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. A leader points proudly at a convoy of AI vehicles leaving the office campus.
2. Each vehicle has a different navigation app and destination: "Reduce cost," "Improve CX," "Automate intake," "Executive demo."
3. Vehicles block each other at shared roads, duplicate routes, and one runs out of token fuel.
4. The Product Lead appears in a lead vehicle with a dispatch map labeled "Destination / Sequence / Dependencies / Reuse."

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: Leader: "Everyone is moving fast."
- Panel 2: Nav labels: "Different destination."
- Panel 3: Driver: "Are we in the same convoy?"
- Panel 4: Caption: "Everyone was moving fast. That was the problem."

## Punchline Options

- Everyone was moving fast. That was the problem.
- A convoy needs more than simultaneous acceleration.
- Motion looked like alignment from the boardroom.

## Image-Generation Notes

The convoy should read as coordinated at first glance, then reveal each vehicle's different destination. Use simple route arrows or navigation screens. The Product Lead should be a lead vehicle or dispatcher, not a superhero.

## Open Questions

- Should the final panel show the Product Lead at a dispatch board instead of inside a lead vehicle?
- Does "lead vehicle" imply too much command-and-control, or is that useful for the metaphor?

# Comic 019: The Pit Crew Becomes Roadside Assistance

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Selected draft generated; signed version available

## Core Message

Without product orchestration, scarce Data Science capacity gets consumed supporting disconnected low-value AI experiments instead of building high-leverage decision systems.

## Core Critique

Advanced technical teams are often treated as a catch-all helpdesk for citizen automations, fragile pilots, unclear requests, and local prototypes. Strategic modeling, optimization, evaluation, and advanced AI work are delayed because the expert team is constantly pulled into roadside support.

## Vehicle Analogy

Data Science is the expert pit crew for high-performance enterprise decision systems. Without intake, standard patterns, and escalation rules, the pit crew becomes roadside assistance for every stalled scooter, golf cart, and homemade prototype.

## Audience

Data Science leaders, analytics executives, AI product leads, citizen development sponsors, and business partners requesting AI support.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. A high-performance enterprise decision vehicle waits in a pit lane labeled "Optimization / Forecasting / Strategic AI."
2. The Data Science pit crew receives a flood of calls from tiny stalled vehicles labeled "HR Macro Bot," "Finance Scooter," and "Legal Intake Cart."
3. The pit crew is shown inflating scooter tires, debugging a golf cart dashboard, and reading a vague prompt request.
4. The Product Lead enters with an "AI Work Routing" board, redirects scooter-tire work to a standard support lane, and sends the Data Science pit crew back toward the strategic decision vehicle.

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: Pit sign: "Data Science: High-Performance Decision Systems."
- Panel 2: Calls: "My bot stalled." "Can you fix this prompt?" "Dashboard smoke?"
- Panel 3: Pit crew member: "Why are we inflating scooter tires?"
- Panel 4: Product Lead board: "AI Work Routing: Self-service kit / Standard pattern / Analytics Engineering / Data Science escalation."
- Panel 4: Product Lead: "Pit crew work goes through the pit lane."

## Punchline Options

- We hired a pit crew, then used them to inflate scooter tires.
- Data Science became roadside assistance because no one designed the service model.
- The expert team was not slow. It was misrouted.

## Image-Generation Notes

The funniest contrast is expertise versus task mismatch. Show the pit crew as competent and focused, not annoyed or elitist. Keep the stalled vehicles intentionally small and local.

Panel 4 should show the Product Lead actively changing the system of work, not personally fixing the scooters. Use a simple routing board labeled "AI Work Routing" with four lanes: "Self-service kit," "Standard pattern," "Analytics Engineering," and "Data Science escalation." In the background, scooter-tire requests are redirected toward a standard support lane while the Data Science pit crew turns back toward the high-performance enterprise decision vehicle.

Keep the routing-board text legible and sparse. The Product Lead should feel operationally calm, not heroic.

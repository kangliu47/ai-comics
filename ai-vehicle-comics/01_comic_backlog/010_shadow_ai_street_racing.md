# Comic 010: Shadow AI Street Racing

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Initial concept

## Core Message

When official AI paths are slow, unusable, or over-controlled, employees will find unofficial tools, creating preventable security and governance risk.

## Core Critique

Shadow AI is often framed only as employee misbehavior. This strip should expose the organizational failure underneath: official tools are too hard to access, too poorly enabled, or too disconnected from real work.

## Vehicle Analogy

Official AI vehicles are trapped in a red-tape garage. Employees street-race unapproved AI cars outside because they still need to move. Data leakage is documents spilling from the trunk.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. Inside the corporate garage, official AI cars are covered in approval forms, caution tape, and signs reading "Pending Review" and "Use Case Intake."
2. Employees wait in line with urgent work while a clerk stamps another form.
3. Outside, employees speed through an alley in flashy unapproved AI cars labeled "Free Tool" and "Personal Account."
4. One unapproved car takes a sharp turn while documents spill from the trunk. Security points at the alley while the official garage remains gridlocked.

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: Garage signs: "Official AI," "Pending Review," "Use Case Intake."
- Panel 2: Employee: "The deadline is today."
- Panel 3: Car labels: "Free Tool," "Personal Account."
- Panel 4: Security: "Why are they out there?" Spilled documents: "Customer Data," "Strategy Deck."

## Punchline Options

- If the official road is unusable, people build side streets.
- Shadow AI is often an adoption failure wearing a security costume.
- People will move. The question is whether they use your roads.

## Image-Generation Notes

Use strong contrast between a frozen, bureaucratic official garage and kinetic unofficial street racing outside. Keep the leak visual simple: a few papers flying from a trunk. Avoid glorifying the unapproved tools; the point is preventable risk, not rebellion as virtue.

## Open Questions

- Should the final line come from security or be a caption?
- Is "street racing" too chaotic for the understated style, or does the absurdity help the joke land?

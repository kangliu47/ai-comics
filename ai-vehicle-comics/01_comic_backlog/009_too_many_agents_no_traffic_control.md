# Comic 009: Too Many Agents, No Traffic Control

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Initial concept

## Core Message

Agent sprawl without governance creates operational chaos, duplicated capabilities, unclear ownership, and unmanaged access risk.

## Core Critique

Enterprises can build or buy agents faster than they can govern them. Without inventory, ownership, access review, traffic rules, and observability, the road fills with autonomous vehicles nobody can safely coordinate.

## Vehicle Analogy

Agents are autonomous vehicles. Governance is traffic control, fleet management, lane markings, inspection, and access oversight.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. A proud team launches an autonomous vehicle labeled "Expense Agent."
2. The camera widens to reveal many similar vehicles: "Expense Agent v2," "Final Travel Agent," "Final Travel Agent Final," "Knowledge Bot," and "Knowledge Bot Revived."
3. The vehicles jam an enterprise campus intersection, honking and blocking each other. A traffic light blinks uselessly.
4. IT stands beside a clipboard and asks: "Which one has payroll access?"

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: Team: "We shipped our first agent!"
- Panel 2: Vehicle labels: "Expense Agent," "Expense Agent v2," "Travel Agent Final," "Knowledge Bot Revived."
- Panel 3: Sign: "Enterprise Agent Lane." Traffic light: "?"
- Panel 4: IT: "Which one has payroll access?"

## Punchline Options

- A thousand vehicles is not a transportation system.
- Agent sprawl is traffic without city planning.
- You built cars faster than governance could paint the lanes.

## Image-Generation Notes

The visual should feel crowded but still legible. Limit vehicle labels to the funniest few. The last panel should focus on the IT/security person's alarm and the payroll-access question.

## Open Questions

- Should the strip include "owner unknown" as a label, or is payroll access enough?
- Is "Final Travel Agent Final" too software-engineering-specific, or exactly the right amount of enterprise absurdity?

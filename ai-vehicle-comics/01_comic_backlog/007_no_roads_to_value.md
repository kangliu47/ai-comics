# Comic 007: No Roads to Value

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

## Status

Initial concept

## Core Message

AI assistants may be capable, but without integrations into enterprise systems and workflows, they cannot reach high-value destinations efficiently.

## Core Critique

Organizations often blame AI tools for underwhelming value when the real obstacle is disconnected systems, missing APIs, brittle workflows, and enterprise roadblocks.

## Vehicle Analogy

AI assistants are cars. Enterprise integrations are roads, bridges, access ramps, and permitted routes. Without route access, every destination feels far away no matter how good the vehicle is or how accurate the map is.

## Candidate Four-Panel Flow

1. A capable AI car idles at a starting line labeled "Assistant."
2. The navigation map correctly identifies destinations: "Email," "CRM," "SharePoint," "Workday," and "Knowledge Base."
3. The route itself is blocked: missing bridges, locked gates, closed ramps, unpaved paths, and disconnected lanes.
4. A manager looks at the stuck car and says, "I guess AI is overhyped."

## Dialogue And Labels

- Panel 1: Car label: "AI Assistant."
- Panel 2: Navigation labels: "CRM," "Email," "Knowledge Base."
- Panel 3: Route signs: "No API," "Bridge Out," "Ramp Closed," "Access Denied."
- Panel 4: Manager: "I guess AI is overhyped."

## Punchline Options

- The car works. The route is not open.
- Models provide power. Integrations create movement.
- Without roads, every destination feels far away.

## Image-Generation Notes

Use a simple map or roadway view. Make the car look ready and capable, while the environment is obviously unusable. Avoid drawing too many systems; three destinations and three obstacles should be enough.

## Open Questions

- Should "SharePoint" be included as a label, or should labels remain generic for longevity?
- Is the final line from management sharper than a caption?

## Overlap Watch

Pairs naturally with Comic 006, but must remain about movement and access across workflows, not data quality itself. Comic 006 is "bad map"; Comic 007 is "good map, no road."

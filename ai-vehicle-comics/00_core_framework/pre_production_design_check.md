# Pre-Production Design Check

Use this checklist before generating or revising any visual comic draft.

The purpose is to make the idea better at the text level before spending an image generation pass. The image prompt should be the result of a clear design, not the place where the design is discovered.

## Required Text-Level Pass

Before drawing, write or review:

1. Core message: one sentence naming the enterprise AI insight.
2. Causal chain: what exact enterprise behavior causes what consequence?
3. Visual contrast: what two things should the viewer compare at a glance?
4. Panel logic: does each panel advance the contradiction?
5. Final consequence: is the last panel earned by the previous panels?
6. Punchline: is it specific enough to repeat in a meeting?
7. Clutter risk: which labels, signs, or props can be removed?
8. Overlap watch: is this distinct from existing comics?

## Design Questions

Ask these before generating an image:

- What is the one visual absurdity this comic depends on?
- Does the visual consequence follow from the setup, or is it random chaos?
- Are we showing the operating-model failure, not just making people look foolish?
- Is security, governance, data, token usage, or integration being represented by the right vehicle device?
- Would a viewer understand the joke in 3-5 seconds?

## Security-Specific Check

For security comics, decide which control is central before drawing:

- Guardrails, shoulders, rumble strips: security requirements built into the road.
- Checkpoints, tire checks, brake checks, inspection stickers: production readiness and stability review.
- Stop signs: hard restricted-data boundaries such as `NO PII`, `NO SECRETS`, or `NO CREDENTIALS`.
- Cameras, sensors, trip logs, control rooms: logging, monitoring, and auditability.
- No-parking or no-storage signage: zero data retention.

Do not collapse all of these into one generic security sign.

## When To Revise Before Drawing

Revise the text design first if:

- the final panel is louder but not clearer;
- the visual consequence is dramatic but not causally earned;
- security looks like bureaucracy rather than road design;
- the panel has too many labels;
- the punchline could apply to any AI comic.

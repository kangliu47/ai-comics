# Reusable Brainstorming Prompt

Fictional industry scenario. People, organizations, workflows, and figures are illustrative.

Paste this into the ChatGPT project when you want help generating new comic backlog ideas.

```text
I am brainstorming new ideas for the AI Vehicle Comics series.

Please use the project context: concise enterprise AI satire through a compact vehicle analogy. I want dry, exact, visually clear four-panel comic ideas for technically literate business leaders and enterprise technology teams.

Focus on one specific enterprise AI failure mode at a time. Do not make generic "AI bad" jokes. Do not over-expand the metaphor into a fictional universe.

For each idea, return:

1. Title
2. Core message
3. Core critique
4. Vehicle analogy
5. Four-panel beat
6. Dialogue and labels
7. Punchline options
8. Image-generation notes
9. Overlap watch against the existing backlog
10. Open questions or risks
11. Pre-production design check: causal chain, visual contrast, final consequence, and clutter risk

Use these analogy rules:

- Models are engines.
- AI products are vehicles.
- Everyday AI assistants are practical sedans.
- Premium Assistant is a Model Y with autopilot for selected business pilot use.
- Coding agents are Cybertrucks or autonomous work vehicles.
- Tokens/context are fuel, battery charge, energy, and range.
- Data foundations are trusted maps and navigation data.
- Integrations are roads, bridges, ramps, and gated route access.
- Enablement is driver's education.
- Oversight is taking over the wheel.
- Governance is traffic safety and fleet management.
- Security requirements are guardrails, median barriers, shoulder lines, and rumble strips.
- PII, secrets, and credentials are restricted cargo or stop-sign boundaries.
- Logging and monitoring are cameras, sensors, trip logs, and control rooms.
- Zero data retention is no vendor parking lot and no overnight storage.

Existing selected/implemented comics:

- 001: No Driver's License, Full Self-Driving Enabled - skipped enablement before autonomous agents.
- 002: Keys Assigned, Nobody Driving - license assignment is not adoption.
- 003: Waiting for the Cybertruck - useful AI sedans exist now, but teams chase agentic glamour.
- 004: The Entire AI Highway Department - one person is assigned to operate the whole enterprise AI highway system.
- 005: The Guardrails Were The Road - security guardrails are production road design, not delay.

Current backlog themes already covered:

- Bad maps/data foundations.
- No roads/integration gaps.
- Prompt injection as forged detour signs.
- Agent sprawl without traffic control.
- Shadow AI street racing.
- Premium Assistant keys without route plans.
- Token range waste.
- Integrated Assistant as the company sedan already parked.
- Pilot group selection versus VIP parking.

Please propose new ideas that are distinct from these. Favor compact, repeatable analogies and punchlines that could be repeated in a meeting.
```

## Public-content boundary

Use fictional industry scenarios and synthetic figures. Refer to people only by role or approved initials; do not document personal facts, private workplace events, employer/client identities, identity mappings, credentials, or local filesystem paths. Review prompts and artwork as public content.

## 2026-06-05: Capability, Access, And Capacity As Operating-Model Pillars

Decision: Add a durable operating-model layer to the vehicle analogy: capability is the skilled AI/data/engineering driver; access is keys, route passes, dispatch pairing, decision rights, and open gates; capacity is lanes, bridges, road crew, merge room, support coverage, and delivery bandwidth.

Rationale: Enterprise AI pilots fail to scale for different reasons that should remain visually distinct. A capable driver blocked at a gate is not the same problem as an approved driver stuck on a tiny pilot lane, and neither is the same problem as an open highway assigned to the wrong driver.

Implications:

- Use locked gates, dispatch booths, route passes, and badge readers for decision rights and business-route access.
- Use narrow pilot lanes, bridge work, road crews, and merge bottlenecks for capacity constraints.
- Use trained drivers, confused assigned operators, manuals, and stalled vehicles for capability gaps.
- Keep the critique on operating-model alignment rather than generic tool failure or individual incompetence.

## 2026-05-19: Separate Tokens, Data, And Integrations

Decision: Use fuel, battery charge, energy, and range for token usage and context budget. Use trusted maps, navigation data, road signs, and destination information for enterprise data foundations. Use roads, bridges, ramps, and gated access for infrastructure and system integration.

Rationale: The previous "data is fuel" metaphor collided with the stronger token-as-energy analogy. Separating these layers gives the series more precision:

- tokens explain cost, scarcity, waste, and range anxiety;
- data foundations explain whether the vehicle knows where to go;
- integrations explain whether the vehicle can physically reach the destination.

Implications:

- Future token comics should show battery/range, charging, fuel burn, heavy cargo, or inefficient trips.
- Future data comics should show bad maps, stale GPS, missing destinations, contradictory road signs, or navigation errors.
- Future integration comics should show roads, bridges, ramps, gates, permissions, and blocked routes.

Decision: The initial chosen art direction is "Minimalist monochrome stick-figure editorial comic, dry professional satire, clean pen-and-ink line work, light grayscale shading, highly legible visual metaphor."

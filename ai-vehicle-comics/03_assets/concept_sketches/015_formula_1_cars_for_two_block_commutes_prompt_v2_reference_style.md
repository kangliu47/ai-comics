# Comic 015 Sketch Prompt V2: Reference Style

This revision keeps the same comic concept but makes the prompt more explicit about speaker placement and bubble ownership. The main design change from v1 is that Panel 1 combines the executive's rollout and "strategic one" language into one executive-owned bubble, reducing the risk of a detached second executive line. Panel 3 also uses strict above-speaker bubble placement so the departing employee, Ops person, and manager each clearly own their line.

## Pre-Production Design Check

- Core message: Premium model access without enablement, tiering, cost controls, and monitoring turns AI adoption into prestige-driven fuel burn.
- Causal chain: leadership distributes top-tier model access as a perk; users apply it to trivial tasks and leave sessions active; scaled usage becomes an enormous AI fuel bill.
- Visual contrast: Formula 1 cars for two-block office errands versus an ignored practical sedan.
- Panel logic: rollout creates prestige, tiny errands reveal mismatch, parked-but-burning shows hidden cost behavior, invoice delivers the consequence.
- Final consequence: the half-billion-dollar bill is earned by the prior panels' repeated overpowered usage and overnight burn.
- Punchline: "We gave everyone a Formula 1 car for a two-block commute" is specific, repeatable, and tied to the image.
- Clutter risk: fewer Panel 1 bubbles, only one banner, four destination/task labels, three token-burn labels, and the invoice amount.
- Overlap watch: distinct from pilot-selection and route-planning comics because the failure mode is premium model tiering, lingering sessions, and cost awareness.

## Image Prompt

Create a square 2x2 editorial comic page in this exact visual style:

Hand-lettered workplace cartoon, minimalist black-and-white editorial comic strip, clean white background, pen-and-ink line art, light gray marker shading, thick slightly imperfect panel borders, simple stick-figure office characters with round heads and spare expressive faces, slightly more rendered grayscale Formula 1 cars and one practical sedan, dry professional satire, readable signage, sparse speech bubbles, uncluttered composition, near-monochrome black-white-gray palette with only tiny muted warning accents if useful.

Overall page layout:
- Large hand-lettered title across the top: "FORMULA 1 CARS FOR TWO-BLOCK COMMUTES"
- Four equal panels in a 2x2 grid.
- Each panel has a black square number badge in the upper-left corner: "1.", "2.", "3.", "4."
- Each panel has one short handwritten narrator caption near the top.
- Keep all text large and legible.
- Use only the text specified below.
- Add a centered bottom kicker below the panels.
- Add a small artist signature at the bottom-right corner: "KKLL"

Global speech bubble rules:
- Every speech bubble must be above or immediately beside the speaker's head, never down by the speaker's feet.
- Every speech bubble must have a clear short tail pointing directly to that speaker's mouth.
- Bubble tails must not cross another person, another bubble, the AI key, the car, or major props.
- Do not leave any speech bubble floating without an obvious speaker.
- Do not attach a speech bubble to the wrong person.
- Keep dialogue bubbles compact, with large readable hand lettering.
- If space is tight, simplify the background before moving speech bubbles away from speakers.

Panel 1:
Narrator caption: "The rollout."
Scene: Corporate office-campus parking-lot launch event. Use a simple left-to-right foreground speaker layout with exactly three foreground speakers: executive on the left, employee in the center, coworker observer on the right. The executive hands the employee an AI key fob labeled "AI". A row of sleek grayscale Formula 1 cars is displayed behind them like a product launch. A plain gray sedan labeled "Standard Copilot" is parked off to the side and ignored.
Banner: "PREMIUM AI ACCESS FOR EVERYONE"
Speech bubbles:
Executive: "Everyone gets premium model access. The strategic one."
Employee: "The fast one?"
Coworker: "You live two blocks away."
Employee: "Now I can go anywhere."
Composition rule: No speech bubble may appear near anyone's feet. Keep the key handoff visible between executive and employee.
Mood: polished corporate transformation language versus tiny actual need.

Panel 2:
Narrator caption: "Tiny errands, maximum horsepower."
Scene: Office courtyard with absurdly short destinations. Employees use Formula 1 cars for tiny office tasks: one drives to a printer, one sits in a car beside a sign for rewriting "Thanks", and one uses a small pit crew to summarize three bullets. The ordinary sedan is available but ignored.
Labels/signs: "Printer", "2 blocks", "Top-Tier Model", "Standard Copilot"
Speech bubbles:
Employee 1: "I drove mine to the printer."
Employee 2: "I rewrote 'Thanks.'"
Employee 3: "Mine summarized three bullets."
Employee 4: "The sedan felt less enterprise."
Mood: straight-faced visual mismatch, not slapstick.

Panel 3:
Narrator caption: "Parked, still burning."
Scene: End of day outside the office. One Formula 1 car is parked on a roller/dyno platform in the center. Rear tires are still spinning ferociously with motion blur, smoke, and light warning marks. The key remains in the car. A token burn gauge and monitor are visible but do not cover speech bubbles.
Speaker layout:
Employee is far left, walking away with briefcase but still close enough that the bubble can sit above the employee's head.
Ops person is center-right, kneeling or leaning near the rear tire and looking at the spinning wheels.
Manager is far right, standing confidently and watching the car.
Labels/monitor text: "Premium model session active", "Token Burn Rate", "Overnight: ACTIVE"
Speech bubbles:
Employee: "All set. I parked it."
Ops: "Why are the rear tires still spinning?"
Manager: "That means it's creating value."
Composition rule: Keep the dyno car and token-burn gauge as the visual center. Put the three speech bubbles in open sky or margins above their speakers, not over the spinning wheels.
Mood: the core metaphor; parked but still consuming resources.

Panel 4:
Narrator caption: "The invoice."
Scene: Executive conference room. Executives, finance, and IT/Ops stare at a giant invoice on a screen. They look stunned but restrained.
Invoice text large: "MONTHLY AI FUEL CHARGES: $500,000,000"
Optional invoice line items if legible: "Model Usage", "Token Consumption", "Overnight Sessions", "Total Due"
Speech bubbles:
Executive: "Half a billion dollars?"
Ops: "F1 cars. Two-block commute. Engines running overnight."
CFO: "Horsepower is not strategy."
Mood: absurd consequence, earned by prior panels.

Bottom kicker centered below panels:
"We gave everyone a Formula 1 car for a two-block commute."

Signature:
"KKLL"

Strict style constraints:
- Monochrome black-and-white only, with light grayscale shading.
- Keep humans as simple stick figures; make vehicles, telemetry screens, invoice, and office signage slightly more detailed.
- The critique should target leadership and operating-model design, not make employees look stupid.
- Avoid real AI vendor logos or direct branding.
- Do not make the image glossy, colorful, cinematic, photorealistic, anime-inspired, superhero-inspired, or sci-fi.
- Do not add extra invented text.
- Do not overcrowd the panels.
- Keep all lettering large, hand-drawn, and readable.

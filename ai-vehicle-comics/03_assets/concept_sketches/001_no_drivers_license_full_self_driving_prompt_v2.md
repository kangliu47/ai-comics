# Comic 001 Sketch Prompt V2

## Creative Tightening

The first sketch proves the base style works. The next pass should make the joke more comic-specific by increasing the contrast between enterprise ceremony and operational incompetence.

## Sharper Comic Logic

The absurdity is not merely that the truck crashes. The absurdity is that leadership treats autonomy as a reason to skip enablement, while the employee still has to define the objective, understand the guardrails, monitor behavior, and know when to take over.

## Panel-Level Joke Design

1. The driver's education classroom should feel abandoned, not merely under-attended. The training topics are the exact skills the later scene needs.
2. The launch ceremony should be overconfident and corporate: ribbon, banner, applause, and a leader pointing at autonomy as if it removes responsibility.
3. The employee should not look reckless. They should look abandoned by the process, staring at a dashboard full of terms leadership never trained them on.
4. The crash should be a consequence of missing oversight, not random slapstick. The signs should read like enterprise failure modes caused by unverified agent action.

## Preferred Punchline

Employee: "I thought autonomous meant I could stop paying attention."

This is sharper than "I thought it was autonomous" because it names the dangerous misunderstanding.

## Image Prompt

Create a four-panel comic strip in this style:

Minimalist black-and-white editorial comic strip, simple stick-figure workplace characters, pen-and-ink line art, hand-drawn outlines, clean white background, light grayscale shading, expressive but spare faces, witty professional satire, visually clear vehicle metaphor, understated rather than flashy, readable signage and dialogue, modern workplace setting, dry humor, clear panel borders, uncluttered composition.

Comic title:
No Driver's License, Full Self-Driving Enabled

Core message:
Enterprise leadership is trying to deploy autonomous AI agents before employees have learned the basic skills required to supervise them.

Visual metaphor:
AI enablement is driver's education. An agent platform is an autonomous work truck. Human oversight is knowing when and how to take the wheel.

Panel 1:
An almost abandoned corporate driver's education classroom labeled "AI ENABLEMENT." A lonely instructor stands beside a whiteboard. Most chairs are empty. One chair has a forgotten coffee cup. Whiteboard text is large and sparse: "PROMPT," "VERIFY," "TAKE OVER," "FAILURE MODES." Instructor dialogue: "Anyone?"

Panel 2:
A corporate launch ceremony outside the building. Executives clap beside a simple angular autonomous work truck labeled "AGENT PLATFORM." There is a ribbon and a banner reading "FULLY AUTONOMOUS FUTURE OF WORK." One executive proudly says: "Great news. No drivers required."

Panel 3:
Inside the truck cab. A nervous employee sits in the driver's seat with both hands hovering awkwardly, not touching the wheel. The dashboard has three large buttons or labels: "OBJECTIVE," "GUARDRAILS," "TAKEOVER." The employee says: "Which one is the approve pedal?"

Panel 4:
The truck has comically plowed through a few simple road signs, not violent, just absurd. Broken signs read "WRONG EMAIL," "UNVERIFIED DATA," and "POLICY EXCEPTION." The employee is gripping the wheel too late and yelling: "I thought autonomous meant I could stop paying attention."

Composition requirements:
- Four equal panels in a single horizontal strip.
- Black-and-white only with light grayscale shading.
- Keep all text large and legible.
- Use only the listed text; do not invent extra labels.
- Make panel 4 the funniest and clearest panel.
- Keep the vehicle simple, angular, and readable as an autonomous work truck.
- Avoid glossy color, cinematic lighting, photorealism, anime style, dense backgrounds, tiny labels, or sci-fi worldbuilding.

# Comic 004 Sketch Prompt V1: Reference Style

This prompt preserves the operating-model critique while making Panel 2 the iconic image: four enterprise AI lanes converging into one person who has been assigned the entire highway department.

## Image Prompt

Create a square 2x2 editorial comic page in this exact visual style:

Hand-lettered workplace cartoon, monochrome black-and-white pen-and-ink line art, clean white background, light gray marker shading, thick slightly imperfect panel borders, simple stick-figure office characters with round heads and spare expressions, more detailed grayscale transportation infrastructure props, road signs, vehicles, blueprints, barricades, dashboard screens, dry professional satire, readable signage, sparse speech bubbles, uncluttered composition.

Overall page layout:
- Large hand-lettered title across the top: "The Entire AI Highway Department"
- Four equal panels in a 2x2 grid.
- Each panel has a black square number badge in the upper-left corner: "1.", "2.", "3.", "4."
- Each panel has one short handwritten narrator caption near the top.
- Keep all text large and legible.
- Use only the text specified below.
- Add a large centered bottom kicker below the panels.
- Add a small artist signature at the bottom-right corner: "AKB + KKLL"

Panel 1:
Narrator caption: "The roadmap looked mature."
Scene: A clean corporate boardroom. A leadership presenter stands beside a large presentation slide. Three simple stick-figure audience members sit at a table with a coffee mug and a sheet of paper.
Speech bubble from leader: "Our enterprise AI strategy is secure, scalable, governed, and production-ready."
Slide header: "ENTERPRISE AI STRATEGY"
Slide subheader: "SECURE. SCALABLE. GOVERNED. PRODUCTION-READY."
Main slide visual: a clean four-lane highway stretching toward a distant road sign.
Distant road sign: "AI TRANSFORMATION EXPRESSWAY"
Lane labels at the bottom:
"AI Platforms"
"Governance"
"Infrastructure"
"Flagship AI Apps"
Mood: orderly, polished, credible, executive.

Panel 2:
Narrator caption: "Behind the slide, the lanes merged."
Scene: The viewer is behind the polished strategy slide. Four road lanes labeled "AI Platforms," "Governance," "Infrastructure," and "Flagship AI Apps" converge into one chaotic but readable junction centered on a single engineer wearing a simple work helmet.
Small sign near top-left: "Behind the strategy slide..."
Central engineer action: the engineer simultaneously holds a traffic paddle labeled "Governance," holds a platform wrench, studies a bridge blueprint on a drafting table labeled "Platform Evaluation," and welds or repairs a half-built vehicle labeled "First AI App."
Nearby sign: "STAFFING PLAN: 1"
Small off-panel line: "Lean team. High leverage."
Platform area: a compact maintenance depot and a short row of boxy AI fleet vehicles.
Depot sign: "Platform Maintenance Depot"
Fleet labels, only if legible:
"Vector DB"
"Model Registry"
"Eval Service"
"LLM Gateway"
Mood: dry absurdity, structurally impossible, not slapstick.

Panel 3:
Narrator caption: "Then the test drive began."
Scene: The same engineer is inside a barely roadworthy, partly assembled vehicle labeled "First AI App," driving toward a curving road that leads to the expressway. The vehicle should look unstable but still readable, not exploded or cartoonishly destroyed.
Speech bubble from engineer: "Which lane am I in?"
Dashboard or surrounding interruptions:
Phone notification: "Access request pending"
Laptop video call: "Architecture Review"
Blueprint partly blocking windshield: "Architecture decision needed"
Dashboard sticky note: "Demo Pending"
Road sign cluster:
"Platform Access"
"Review Exit"
"Platform Approval"
Mood: distracted driving as operating model, unsafe because attention is fragmented.

Panel 4:
Narrator caption: "The scaling problem arrived on schedule."
Scene: A road sign reads "AI TRANSFORMATION EXPRESSWAY," but the entrance ramp is blocked by a large striped barricade.
Barricade label: "ON-RAMP CLOSED"
Foreground: the engineer is mostly buried under a mound of enterprise artifacts, still visible enough to read as competent but overloaded.
Artifact labels:
"Platform runbooks"
"Governance policies"
"Review Packets"
"Architecture blueprints"
"Data classification guide"
"Security controls matrix"
"Risk assessment"
"Model evaluation plan"
Nearby: the incomplete "First AI App" vehicle sits stalled.
Two leadership figures stand at the side facing the blocked on-ramp.
Speech bubble from leader: "Why aren't we scaling faster?"
Mood: sincere leadership surprise at a throughput problem created by the operating model.

Bottom kicker centered below panels:
"The AI roadmap had four lanes. The staffing plan had one person."

Signature:
"AKB + KKLL"

Strict style constraints:
- Monochrome black-and-white only, with light grayscale shading.
- Keep humans as simple stick figures; make systems props, vehicles, signs, and blueprints slightly more detailed.
- The engineer should look strained but competent, not tragic, heroic, or foolish.
- Use one clean visual absurdity per panel.
- Do not make the scene angry, chaotic, glossy, cinematic, photorealistic, anime-inspired, or sci-fi.
- Do not add extra invented text.
- Do not overcrowd the panels.
- Keep all lettering large, hand-drawn, and readable.

# Comic 005 Sketch Prompt V3: Reference Style

This version uses the pre-production design check. The causal chain is: the fast team skips security and stability checks, the unchecked tire comes loose, and the missing guardrails turn a preventable defect into a cliff failure. The image should be dramatic but controlled, not chaotic.

## Pre-Production Design Check

- Core message: Security checks and guardrails are production infrastructure, not delay.
- Causal chain: skipped inspection causes an unstable wheel; no guardrails turn the instability into a cliff fall.
- Visual contrast: ordinary inspected car on a guarded route versus shiny uninspected race car on an unguarded shortcut.
- Final consequence: the race car falls because the tire failure shown in Panel 3 meets the missing guardrails.
- Clutter risk: use one loose tire, a few papers, one binder, and a few parts; avoid a sky full of debris.

## Image Prompt

Create a square 2x2 editorial comic page in this exact visual style:

Hand-lettered workplace cartoon, monochrome black-and-white pen-and-ink line art, clean white background, light gray marker shading, thick slightly imperfect panel borders, simple stick-figure office characters with round heads and spare expressions, more detailed grayscale road infrastructure, guardrails, inspection signs, traffic cameras, ordinary cars, one sleek AI race car, dry professional satire, readable signage, sparse speech bubbles, uncluttered composition.

Overall page layout:
- Large hand-lettered title across the top: "The Guardrails Were The Road"
- Four equal panels in a 2x2 grid.
- Each panel has a black square number badge in the upper-left corner: "1.", "2.", "3.", "4."
- Each panel has one short handwritten narrator caption near the top.
- Keep all text large and legible.
- Use only the text specified below.
- Add a large centered bottom kicker below the panels.
- Add a small artist signature at the bottom-right corner: "TT + KKLL"

Panel 1:
Narrator caption: "Two teams picked a route."
Scene: A fork in the road. On the left, one modest ordinary AI car enters a road with clear guardrails and a calm approved-route sign. On the right, one sleek AI race car points toward a narrow cliffside shortcut with no guardrails. The ordinary car should look maintained and roadworthy, not weak.
Left road sign: "Approved AI Route"
Guardrail label: "Security Requirements"
Right road sign: "Cliffside Shortcut"
Sleek race car label: "Advanced AI"
Speech bubble from race car driver: "We'll be faster."
Mood: tempting shortcut versus boring safe route.

Panel 2:
Narrator caption: "The approved car got checked."
Scene: A simple security inspection bay on the approved route. A stick-figure inspector checks the ordinary car's wheel bolts or tire with a wrench. Another small mark shows a brake check or inspection sticker. Guardrails continue behind the inspection lane.
Checklist labels:
"Wheel check"
"Brake check"
"NO PII"
"Monitoring"
"Trip Log"
Speech bubble from passenger: "Do we need all this?"
Speech bubble from inspector: "Only if you want the wheels to stay on."
Mood: dry, practical readiness check.

Panel 3:
Narrator caption: "The shortcut skipped inspection."
Scene: The sleek AI race car speeds along a bare cliffside road. The road has no guardrails, no shoulder, and a broken or absent camera pole. One front tire is visibly wobbling loose, with a few motion lines and maybe one loose bolt. Keep the panel clean.
Road sign: "Shortcut to Production"
Sign: "Security Check Skipped"
Warning sign: "NO GUARDRAILS"
Small sign near broken camera pole: "No monitoring"
Speech bubble from driver: "Great velocity."
Mood: fast, confident, visibly unstable.

Panel 4:
Narrator caption: "Production was on the guarded route."
Scene: Foreground: the ordinary inspected car arrives safely at a destination sign on the guarded road. The drivers look around with deadpan confusion. Background: the sleek race car is falling off the cliff because the loose tire has flown off. Show one flying tire, a few papers, one binder, a few small parts, and a broken road edge. The background fall should be obvious and dramatic, but controlled and readable.
Destination sign: "Production Value"
Speech bubble from ordinary driver: "Where did the fast team go?"
Mood: deadpan foreground, earned dramatic consequence in the background.

Bottom kicker centered below panels:
"The guardrails were not the delay. They were the road."

Signature:

Strict style constraints:
- Monochrome black-and-white only, with light grayscale shading.
- Keep humans as simple stick figures; make cars, guardrails, inspection tools, signs, traffic cameras, and road geometry slightly more detailed.
- Security should read as production readiness and road design, not bureaucracy.
- Panel 4 must clearly show the race car falling because a tire came off, but with controlled debris: one tire, a few papers, one binder, and a few small parts.
- Do not make Panel 4 chaotic or crowded.
- Do not make security look like the villain.
- Do not add extra invented text.
- Keep all lettering large, hand-drawn, and readable.

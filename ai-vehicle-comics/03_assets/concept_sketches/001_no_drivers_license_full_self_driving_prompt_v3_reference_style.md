# Comic 001 Sketch Prompt V3: Reference Style

This prompt is tuned to match the successful sample style: square 2x2 editorial comic page, numbered panels, narrator captions, stick-figure workplace characters, more rendered grayscale vehicle, and bottom kicker.

## Why This Prompt Should Work Better

The image model needs the comic's timing, not just the scene. This version gives each panel a narrator caption that carries the satirical logic:

1. The class was optional.
2. Leadership launched autonomy anyway.
3. Untrained employees were put in the seat.
4. The organization learned the lesson after the crash.

## Image Prompt

Create a square 2x2 editorial comic page in this exact visual style:

Hand-lettered workplace cartoon, black-and-white pen-and-ink line art, clean white background, light gray marker shading, thick slightly imperfect panel borders, simple stick-figure office characters with round heads and expressive faces, a slightly more rendered angular grayscale autonomous work truck, dry professional satire, readable signage, sparse speech bubbles, uncluttered composition, near-monochrome black-white-gray palette with only tiny muted orange warning accents for cones or sparks if useful.

Overall page layout:
- Large hand-lettered title across the top: "No Driver's License, Full Self-Driving Enabled"
- Four equal panels in a 2x2 grid.
- Each panel has a black square number badge in the upper-left corner: "1.", "2.", "3.", "4."
- Each panel has one short handwritten narrator caption near the top.
- Keep panel text large and legible.
- Use only the text specified below.
- Add a centered two-line bottom kicker below the panels.

Panel 1:
Narrator caption: "The AI enablement class was... optional."
Scene: Mostly empty corporate training classroom. A lonely stick-figure instructor stands near a whiteboard. Several empty chairs face the board.
Whiteboard title: "AI ENABLEMENT 101"
Whiteboard bullets:
"How to Prompt"
"How to Verify"
"When to Take Over"
"What Could Go Wrong"
Speech bubble from instructor: "Anyone?"
Mood: quiet, awkward, obviously abandoned.

Panel 2:
Narrator caption: "But leadership was excited to launch autonomous agents."
Scene: Corporate launch ceremony. Three cheerful executives clap and wave beside an angular autonomous work truck. Confetti is sparse. A banner hangs above the truck.
Banner text: "THE FUTURE OF WORK!"
Small banner subtext: "FULLY AUTONOMOUS."
Truck side label: "AGENT PLATFORM"
Speech bubble from leader: "Everyone's in!"
Mood: ceremonial confidence with mild corporate absurdity.

Panel 3:
Narrator caption: "Then we put people who'd never driven... in the self-driving seat."
Scene: Interior cab close-up. A nervous employee sits behind the wheel, confused. The dashboard monitor is visible.
Employee thought bubble: "Which pedal is \"approve\"?"
Dashboard title: "AGENT CONTROLS"
Dashboard checklist:
"Objective"
"Context"
"Guardrails"
"Monitoring"
"Takeover"
Small employee mutter near dashboard: "...huh?"
Mood: not reckless, just clearly unsupported.

Panel 4:
Narrator caption: "And that's how we learned: autonomy without readiness is a crash."
Scene: The autonomous work truck has comically crashed through a few enterprise failure signs. The employee looks shocked in the cab. Use motion lines, light smoke, traffic cones, and scattered papers, but keep the drawing readable.
Employee thought bubble: "I thought it was autonomous!"
Broken road signs:
"WRONG EMAIL"
"BAD SUMMARY"
"UNVERIFIED DATA"
"POLICY EXCEPTION"
Mood: absurd consequence, not violence.

Bottom kicker centered below panels:
"AI tools are powerful. People are essential."
"Enable the driver. Then hand over the steering wheel."

Strict style constraints:
- Do not make it glossy, colorful, cinematic, photorealistic, anime-inspired, or sci-fi.
- Do not add extra invented text.
- Do not overcrowd the panels.
- Keep all lettering large, hand-drawn, and readable.
- The vehicle may have more detail than the people, but it must remain a simple editorial cartoon object.

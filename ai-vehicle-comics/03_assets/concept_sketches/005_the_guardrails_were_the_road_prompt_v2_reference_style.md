# Comic 005 Sketch Prompt V2: Reference Style

This version strengthens Panel 4. The race cars on the unguarded shortcut should very clearly fall off the cliff, with papers, binders, cargo, and parts scattering through the air. The joke should be more visually dramatic while keeping the foreground deadpan.

## Image Prompt

Create a square 2x2 editorial comic page in this exact visual style:

Hand-lettered workplace cartoon, monochrome black-and-white pen-and-ink line art, clean white background, light gray marker shading, thick slightly imperfect panel borders, simple stick-figure office characters with round heads and spare expressions, more detailed grayscale road infrastructure, guardrails, signs, traffic cameras, ordinary cars, sleek race cars, dry professional satire, readable signage, sparse speech bubbles, uncluttered composition.

Overall page layout:
- Large hand-lettered title across the top: "The Guardrails Were The Road"
- Four equal panels in a 2x2 grid.
- Each panel has a black square number badge in the upper-left corner: "1.", "2.", "3.", "4."
- Each panel has one short handwritten narrator caption near the top.
- Keep all text large and legible.
- Use only the text specified below.
- Add a large centered bottom kicker below the panels.
- Add a small artist signature at the bottom-right corner: "TT + KKLL"

Panel 1:
Narrator caption: "Two teams picked a route."
Scene: A fork in the road. On the left, two modest ordinary AI cars enter a road with clear guardrails. On the right, sleek race cars point toward a narrow cliffside shortcut with no guardrails. Keep the cars simple but visibly different.
Left road sign: "Approved AI Route"
Guardrail label: "Security Requirements"
Right road sign: "Cliffside Shortcut"
Sleek race car label: "Advanced AI"
Speech bubble from race car driver: "We'll be faster."
Mood: tempting shortcut versus boring safe road.

Panel 2:
Narrator caption: "The approved road had rules."
Scene: The ordinary cars drive calmly along the guarded route. Guardrails run along both sides. Add one traffic camera and one small trip-log booth or clipboard stand.
Road signs:
"NO PII"
"NO SECRETS"
"Monitoring"
"Trip Log"
Speech bubble from passenger: "This route feels slower."
Mood: mild impatience, but the route is clearly engineered.

Panel 3:
Narrator caption: "The shortcut looked efficient."
Scene: The sleek race cars speed along a bare cliffside road. The road has no guardrails, no shoulder, and a broken or absent camera pole. Show speed lines, but keep the panel clean.
Road sign: "Shortcut to Production"
Warning sign: "NO GUARDRAILS"
Small sign near broken camera pole: "No monitoring"
Mood: fast, confident, visibly unsafe.

Panel 4:
Narrator caption: "Production was on the guarded route."
Scene: Foreground: the ordinary cars arrive safely at a destination sign. Drivers look around with deadpan confusion. Background: the unguarded cliffside shortcut is visibly collapsing, and the sleek race cars are clearly falling off the cliff. Show the cars airborne or tumbling downward, with loose papers, binders, boxes, laptop shapes, wheels, and road fragments scattering across the sky. This background crash should be dramatic and unmistakable, but not gory or realistic injury.
Destination sign: "Production Value"
Speech bubble from ordinary driver: "Where did the fast team go?"
Mood: deadpan foreground, dramatic absurd background consequence.

Bottom kicker centered below panels:
"The guardrails were not the delay. They were the road."

Signature:
"TT + KKLL"

Strict style constraints:
- Monochrome black-and-white only, with light grayscale shading.
- Keep humans as simple stick figures; make cars, guardrails, signs, traffic cameras, road geometry, falling cargo, and cliff fragments slightly more detailed.
- Security should read as production road design, not bureaucracy.
- Panel 4 must clearly show the race cars falling off the cliff with scattered papers, binders, boxes, parts, and road fragments in the air.
- The fall should be visually dramatic, but not bloody, not realistic injury, and not disaster spectacle.
- Do not make security look like the villain.
- Do not add extra invented text.
- Do not overcrowd the first three panels.
- Keep all lettering large, hand-drawn, and readable.

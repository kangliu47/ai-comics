User requested closer visual consistency with earlier published/selected work and supplied an editorial review recommending targeted changes while preserving roughly 85% of the design.

### Message

Decentralized creation succeeds locally so convincingly that organizations overlook the coordination infrastructure it has outpaced. Data governance reduced some spreadsheet inconsistencies; it did not eliminate fragmentation. The comic's historical shorthand must not imply that individual productivity failed.

### Analogy

Keep individually functional app vehicles and shared enterprise gridlock. Add one uninstalled traffic signal in its open shipping crate, with a single sign reading “App Governance: Under Construction.” Traffic control represents the missing governance function; roads remain integrations. No broken bridge, crash, rescuer, or proposed solution is needed.

### Four-Panel Beat

1. Preserve the proud spreadsheet users and versioned client filenames.
2. Preserve the delayed, partial coordination response and “Eventually.”
3. Preserve cars emerging from AI laptop workstations; change the narrator to sincere corporate optimism.
4. Preserve checked vehicles blocking each other; show traffic control not yet installed and a manager defending app-level success.

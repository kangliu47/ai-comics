# Comic 003 Style Comparison Prompts

These prompts generated the two comparison drafts for "Waiting for the Cybertruck."

## Current Editorial Minimalist Prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: project concept sketch for AI Vehicle Comics style comparison
Primary request: Create a square 2x2 editorial comic page for the comic idea "Waiting for the Cybertruck" using the CURRENT minimalist monochrome editorial-cartoon approach.

Style/medium: Hand-lettered workplace cartoon, black-and-white pen-and-ink line art, clean white background, light gray marker shading, thick slightly imperfect panel borders, simple stick-figure office characters with round heads and expressive faces, simple grayscale practical sedans, dry professional satire, readable signage, sparse speech bubbles, uncluttered composition, near-monochrome black-white-gray palette with only tiny muted orange warning accents if useful.

Overall page layout:
- Large hand-lettered title across the top: "Waiting for the Cybertruck"
- Four equal panels in a 2x2 grid.
- Each panel has a black square number badge in the upper-left corner: "1.", "2.", "3.", "4."
- Each panel has one short handwritten narrator caption near the top.
- Keep panel text large and legible.
- Use only the text specified below.
- Add a centered two-line bottom kicker below the panels.

Panel 1:
Narrator caption: "The useful cars were already parked outside."
Scene: A simple office parking lot with several ordinary sedans available and easy to enter.
Lot sign: "AVAILABLE NOW"
Car labels: "CHAT ASSISTANT", "DRAFT HELPER", "RESEARCH AID", "MEETING NOTES"
Mood: boring, practical, ready.

Panel 2:
Narrator caption: "But the trend felt bigger down the road."
Scene: A group of employees stands at the curb, looking far down an empty road. They are not confused; they are waiting with exaggerated seriousness.
Roadside sign: "WAITING FOR AUTONOMOUS AGENT CYBERTRUCK"
Speech bubble from employee: "We're waiting for the real AI."
Mood: trend-chasing patience.

Panel 3:
Narrator caption: "One person tried the boring option."
Scene: A practical employee gets into a modest sedan labeled "USEFUL AI TODAY" and drives toward a simple sign.
Road sign: "REAL WORK"
Speech bubble from practical employee: "I just need to get work done."
Mood: quiet competence.

Panel 4:
Narrator caption: "The future was still backordered."
Scene: The waiting group remains at the curb staring down the road. The practical sedan has returned, trunk open with tidy workplace deliverables.
Roadside sign: "BACKORDERED: REVOLUTIONARY FUTURE"
Trunk labels: "DRAFT", "SUMMARY", "ANALYSIS", "DECK"
Mood: dry contrast, no chaos.

Bottom kicker centered below panels:
"Useful today beats revolutionary someday."
"You do not need a Cybertruck to buy groceries."

Constraints:
- Make the ordinary sedans intentionally boring and effective.
- The waiting employees can drive; they are dismissing practical value, not lacking basic training.
- Do not make the Cybertruck visually dominant; it should be absent or only implied by the sign.
- Do not make it glossy, colorful, cinematic, photorealistic, anime-inspired, or sci-fi.
- Do not add extra invented text.
- Do not overcrowd the panels.
- Vehicles may have more detail than people, but remain simple editorial cartoon objects.
```

## First-Principles Deadpan Prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: project concept sketch for AI Vehicle Comics style comparison
Primary request: Create a square 2x2 editorial comic page for the comic idea "Waiting for the Cybertruck" using the proposed FIRST-PRINCIPLES DEADPAN approach.

Style/medium: minimalist monochrome first-principles editorial cartoon, "spherical cow in a vacuum" applied to enterprise AI, clean whiteboard thought-experiment drawing, black-and-white pen-and-ink line art, sparse stick-figure humans, more specific system objects, diagrammatic props, causal mechanism made visible, dry analytical mockery, spare understated absurdity, clean panel borders, light grayscale only for emphasis.

Visual philosophy:
- Simplify reality aggressively.
- Preserve the causal mechanism.
- Make the flawed premise visible as a simple system diagram.
- People are small participants in the absurd system, not the aesthetic focus.
- Vehicles, signs, waiting line, parking lot, delivery trunk, and empty road carry the joke.
- Humor should expose overconfidence and hype with deadpan precision.

Overall page layout:
- Large hand-lettered title across the top: "Waiting for the Cybertruck"
- Four equal panels in a 2x2 grid.
- Each panel has a black square number badge in the upper-left corner: "1.", "2.", "3.", "4."
- Each panel has one short handwritten narrator caption near the top.
- Use diagram-like composition with arrows, simple labels, and exaggeratedly empty space.
- Keep all text large and legible.
- Use only the text specified below.
- Add a centered two-line bottom kicker below the panels.

Panel 1:
Narrator caption: "Several useful cars existed."
Scene: A whiteboard-simple parking lot diagram: four identical boring sedans sit in marked spaces. A huge clean label points to them: "AVAILABLE NOW". A tiny office worker stands beside them holding a laptop, ignored.
Car labels: "CHAT", "DRAFT", "RESEARCH", "NOTES"
Mood: absurdly obvious supply.

Panel 2:
Narrator caption: "The strategy was to wait."
Scene: A long empty road drawn as a ruler-straight line into blank white space. A crowd of tiny stick-figure executives waits behind a velvet rope at the curb, solemn and self-satisfied. A sign points down the empty road: "FUTURE CYBERTRUCK". Add a small clipboard label near the crowd: "VISION".
Speech bubble from executive: "This is more strategic."
Mood: deadpan institutional confidence.

Panel 3:
Narrator caption: "One person tested the premise."
Scene: A tiny practical worker drives one plain sedan along a short arrow from "TASK" to "DONE". The road is only a few feet long, like a clean causal diagram. The waiting crowd is visible in the background, still looking at the empty long road.
Car label: "USEFUL AI TODAY"
Road labels: "TASK" and "DONE"
Speech bubble: "This appears to move."
Mood: analytical undercut.

Panel 4:
Narrator caption: "The waiting continued successfully."
Scene: The same executives are still at the velvet rope, proudly pointing at a dashboard that says "FUTURE READINESS: 100%" while the plain sedan has returned with a trunk full of deliverables. A massive roadside sign says "BACKORDERED". The empty road remains perfectly empty.
Trunk labels: "DRAFT", "SUMMARY", "ANALYSIS", "DECK"
Mood: mockery through calm system absurdity, not slapstick.

Bottom kicker centered below panels:
"The present failed the vibe check."
"So they waited for a vehicle they did not need."

Constraints:
- Make the First-Principles Deadpan version visibly more diagrammatic, more mocking, and more absurd than the current style version.
- Keep humans small, sparse, and expressive: misplaced confidence, self-satisfaction, mild confusion.
- Props and system objects must dominate: road, signs, sedans, dashboard, arrows, labels, waiting rope.
- Do not make it glossy, colorful, cinematic, photorealistic, anime-inspired, or sci-fi.
- No random slapstick, explosions, or chaotic crashes.
- Do not add extra invented text.
- Do not overcrowd panels.
```

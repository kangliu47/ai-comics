# Comic 003 Sketch Prompt V1: Reference Style

This prompt sharpens the Cybertruck concept as trend-chasing rather than basic AI illiteracy. The employees can use practical AI sedans; the joke is that they dismiss useful current tools while waiting for the glamorous coding-agent/autonomous future vehicle.

## Image Prompt

Create a square 2x2 editorial comic page in this exact visual style:

Hand-lettered workplace cartoon, black-and-white pen-and-ink line art, clean white background, light gray marker shading, thick slightly imperfect panel borders, simple stick-figure office characters with round heads and expressive faces, simple grayscale practical sedans, dry professional satire, readable signage, sparse speech bubbles, uncluttered composition, near-monochrome black-white-gray palette with only tiny muted orange warning accents if useful.

Overall page layout:
- Large hand-lettered title across the top: "Waiting for the Cybertruck"
- Four equal panels in a 2x2 grid.
- Each panel has a black square number badge in the upper-left corner: "1.", "2.", "3.", "4."
- Each panel has one short handwritten narrator caption near the top.
- Keep panel text large and legible.
- Use only the text specified below.
- Add a centered two-line bottom kicker below the panels.

Panel 1:
Narrator caption: "The useful cars were already parked outside."
Scene: A simple office parking lot with several ordinary sedans available and easy to enter.
Lot sign: "AVAILABLE NOW"
Car labels:
"CHAT ASSISTANT"
"DRAFT HELPER"
"RESEARCH AID"
"MEETING NOTES"
Mood: boring, practical, ready.

Panel 2:
Narrator caption: "But the trend felt bigger down the road."
Scene: A group of employees stands at the curb, looking far down an empty road. They are not confused; they are waiting with exaggerated seriousness.
Roadside sign: "WAITING FOR AUTONOMOUS AGENT CYBERTRUCK"
Speech bubble from employee: "We're waiting for the real AI."
Mood: trend-chasing patience.

Panel 3:
Narrator caption: "One person tried the boring option."
Scene: A practical employee gets into a modest sedan labeled "USEFUL AI TODAY" and drives toward a simple sign.
Road sign: "REAL WORK"
Speech bubble from practical employee: "I just need to get work done."
Mood: quiet competence.

Panel 4:
Narrator caption: "The future was still backordered."
Scene: The waiting group remains at the curb staring down the road. The practical sedan has returned, trunk open with tidy workplace deliverables.
Roadside sign: "BACKORDERED: REVOLUTIONARY FUTURE"
Trunk labels:
"DRAFT"
"SUMMARY"
"ANALYSIS"
"DECK"
Mood: dry contrast, no chaos.

Bottom kicker centered below panels:
"Useful today beats revolutionary someday."
"You do not need a Cybertruck to buy groceries."

Strict style constraints:
- Make the ordinary sedans look intentionally boring and effective.
- The waiting employees can drive; they are dismissing practical value, not lacking basic training.
- Do not make the Cybertruck visually dominant; it should be absent or only implied by the sign.
- Do not make it glossy, colorful, cinematic, photorealistic, anime-inspired, or sci-fi.
- Do not add extra invented text.
- Do not overcrowd the panels.
- Keep all lettering large, hand-drawn, and readable.
- The vehicles may have more detail than the people, but they must remain simple editorial cartoon objects.

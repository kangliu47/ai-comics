# Comic 019 Prompt V1: Reference Style

This prompt turns the pit-crew concept into a selected draft. The key design choice is that the final panel does not merely show the failure state. It shows the AI Product Lead changing the operating model with an `AI Work Routing` board, while preserving the sharp scooter-tire punchline.

Signature: use the signed selected draft with visible KKLL credit.

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: selected draft comic for the AI Vehicle Comics series
Primary request: Create a polished four-panel editorial comic titled "The Pit Crew Becomes Roadside Assistance" about Data Science being misrouted into low-value AI prototype support until a AI Product Lead creates clear routing.

Style/medium: minimalist black-and-white editorial comic strip; simple stick-figure workplace characters; pen-and-ink line art; hand-drawn outlines; light grayscale marker shading; clean white background; dry professional satire; modern enterprise workplace setting. Square 2x2 comic page with a large hand-lettered title at top, four bordered panels with thick slightly imperfect black borders, black square numbered badges in the upper-left of each panel, one short handwritten narrator caption at the top of each panel, sparse speech bubbles, and a centered bottom kicker outside the panels.

Exact page title: "THE PIT CREW BECOMES ROADSIDE ASSISTANCE"

Panel 1 caption: "The expert team had a lane."
Panel 1 scene: A clean pit lane labeled "DATA SCIENCE" beside a sleek but not futuristic enterprise decision vehicle labeled "STRATEGIC DECISION SYSTEM". A calm, competent pit crew waits with tools. A small pit sign says "Optimization / Forecasting / Strategic AI". Keep labels large and legible.

Panel 2 caption: "Then every prototype called for help."
Panel 2 scene: The pit crew receives many support calls from tiny stalled vehicles on the roadside: a small scooter labeled "HR BOT", a golf cart labeled "FINANCE DASHBOARD", and a little cart labeled "LEGAL INTAKE". Use simple call bubbles: "My bot stalled" and "Dashboard smoke". Make the tiny vehicles amusing but not chaotic.

Panel 3 caption: "The pit crew became roadside assistance."
Panel 3 scene: The Data Science pit crew is inflating a tiny scooter tire, checking a smoking golf cart dashboard, and holding a vague ticket labeled "make it AI". One pit crew member says: "Why are we inflating scooter tires?" The high-performance decision vehicle is visible waiting in the background.

Panel 4 caption: "Product leadership fixed the routing."
Panel 4 scene: A calm AI Product Lead enters with a clipboard and a simple routing board labeled "AI WORK ROUTING". The board has four short lanes: "Self-service kit", "Standard pattern", "Analytics Eng", "Data Science escalation". In the background, scooter-tire requests are redirected toward a small standard support lane while the Data Science pit crew turns back toward the strategic decision vehicle. The Product Lead says: "Pit crew work goes through the pit lane."

Bottom kicker text, centered and large: "We hired a pit crew, then used them to inflate scooter tires."

Composition/framing: each panel uncluttered, highly readable at small size, with the visual joke clear in 3-5 seconds. The Product Lead should feel operationally calm, not heroic. Data Science should look competent, not annoyed or elitist.

Constraints: exact text should be as legible as possible. Keep all labels short. Use near-monochrome black, white, and gray. No glossy color, no photorealism, no anime, no superheroes, no sci-fi worldbuilding, no dense tiny text, no logo, no watermark.
```

# Comic 002 Sketch Prompt V1: Reference Style

This prompt reframes the original Copilot-license parking-garage idea around license assignment as KPI theater. The key visual distinction is that employees have AI-tool key fobs, but still do not know where to drive, why to drive, or how the tools fit their work.

## Image Prompt

Create a square 2x2 editorial comic page in this exact visual style:

Hand-lettered workplace cartoon, black-and-white pen-and-ink line art, clean white background, light gray marker shading, thick slightly imperfect panel borders, simple stick-figure office characters with round heads and expressive faces, simple grayscale corporate fleet sedans, dry professional satire, readable signage, sparse speech bubbles, uncluttered composition, near-monochrome black-white-gray palette with only tiny muted blue or green dashboard accents if useful.

Overall page layout:
- Large hand-lettered title across the top: "Keys Assigned, Nobody Driving"
- Four equal panels in a 2x2 grid.
- Each panel has a black square number badge in the upper-left corner: "1.", "2.", "3.", "4."
- Each panel has one short handwritten narrator caption near the top.
- Keep panel text large and legible.
- Use only the text specified below.
- Add a centered two-line bottom kicker below the panels.

Panel 1:
Narrator caption: "The adoption KPI looked excellent."
Scene: Corporate leadership at a small launch table handing identical key fobs to a line of employees. A dashboard screen is beside them.
Dashboard text: "AI ADOPTION KPI"
Dashboard subtext: "100% LICENSES ASSIGNED"
Speech bubble from leader: "Everyone gets a key!"
Mood: cheerful corporate measurement theater.

Panel 2:
Narrator caption: "The fleet was technically available."
Scene: A corporate parking garage full of simple parked sedans. The cars are parked neatly and motionless.
Car labels:
"COPILOT"
"CHATGPT"
"INTERNAL ASSISTANT"
Small wall sign: "DRIVER TRAINING: TBD"
Mood: abundant but inert.

Panel 3:
Narrator caption: "But nobody explained the destination."
Scene: Employees walk on the sidewalk outside the garage holding key fobs, passing the garage entrance instead of entering. They look mildly puzzled, not panicked.
Speech bubble from employee 1: "Where are we supposed to go?"
Speech bubble from employee 2: "Which one helps with my job?"
Mood: practical confusion.

Panel 4:
Narrator caption: "So the dashboard called it adoption."
Scene: Leadership proudly points at a large KPI dashboard while the full garage is visible behind them. Employees are still walking past outside.
Dashboard text: "10,000 KEYS ISSUED"
Speech bubble from leader: "Adoption complete."
Mood: dry absurdity, motionless fleet.

Bottom kicker centered below panels:
"Keys assigned. Nobody driving."
"Access is not adoption."

Strict style constraints:
- Do not depict a legal driver's license card; the "license" should feel like enterprise software access.
- Do not make employees look incapable or foolish; make the system unsupported.
- Do not make it glossy, colorful, cinematic, photorealistic, anime-inspired, or sci-fi.
- Do not add extra invented text.
- Do not overcrowd the panels.
- Keep all lettering large, hand-drawn, and readable.
- The vehicles may have more detail than the people, but they must remain simple editorial cartoon objects.
